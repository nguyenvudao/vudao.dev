## Whose opinions these are

Mine. Everything here is written in a personal capacity. Nothing on this site is a statement by any
employer of mine, past or present, and nothing here should be read as one.

## No sponsored content

I don't publish paid posts, sponsored reviews, or affiliate links. Nobody pays for a mention here.
If that ever changes, the post in question will say so at the top.

## Mistakes

These posts are notes from my own work, written at a point in time. Some of them are years old and
the tooling has moved on. Expect errors, and please tell me about them — email me via the *Contact*
link in the footer, or open an issue on
[the repository](https://github.com/nguyenvudao/vudao.dev/issues).

## Corrections and old posts

I don't quietly delete posts that turned out to be wrong or outdated; I'd rather leave them up and
correct them, since people may have linked to them. Small fixes (typos, dead links, formatting) I
just make. Anything that changes the substance of a post gets a note in the post itself.

Old URLs from previous versions of this blog still redirect to their current location.
