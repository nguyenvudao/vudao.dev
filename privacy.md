This is a personal blog. I collect as little as possible — but this page is honest about the third
parties involved, because a static site still leaks your IP address to whoever serves its assets.

## What I don't do

- **No analytics.** No Google Analytics, no GoatCounter, no page-view counter, no heatmaps.
- **No ads and no ad network.**
- **No newsletter and no mailing list**, so there is no list to be on.
- **No account, no login, no profile.** Nothing on this site asks you to identify yourself.

## Hosting

The site is served by **GitHub Pages**. GitHub processes your IP address in its server logs to serve
the pages and to protect the service against abuse. See the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Fonts and scripts loaded from third parties

Pages load web fonts from **Google Fonts** (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) and a few
JavaScript libraries from the **jsDelivr** CDN (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`). Your browser contacts those
hosts directly, so they receive your IP address and user agent. Their policies:
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy),
[jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).

## Comments

Comments are powered by **Disqus**, which loads only when you scroll to the comment area. Disqus
sets its own cookies and processes whatever you type, plus the name and email address you enter — it
is the data controller for that, not me. See the
[Disqus Privacy Policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).

Guest comments (no account needed) require an email address. I can see it while moderating and I
don't use it for anything else.

## In your browser

The site registers a service worker so pages work offline. It stores a copy of pages and assets in
your browser's own cache. Nothing is sent anywhere; clearing site data removes it.

## Contact

Questions, or want something removed? Email me — the address is behind the *Contact* link in the
footer.
